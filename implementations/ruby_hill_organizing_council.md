# Charter for the Ruby Hill Organizing Council

# Vision
To build a better world.

# Mission
Starting here in Ruby Hill, we want to organize and activate those who live in the neighborhood into a political force that is capable of making the changes we want to see in the world.

# Core Values
1) Don't be a dick.
1) No tolerance of any sort for hate and discrimination such as racism, sexism, ableism, transphobia, etc.
1) No stupid questions, asking questions is how we learn from each other.
1) We are here to have fun & make something cool, not get rich and steal from each other.

# Points of Unity
The following are our political points of unity in no particular order
* Medicare For All - Universal healthcare w /no premiums, co-pays, or deductibles. Guarantees for reproductive & gender affirming care.
* End the War On Drugs - Treat drug addiction as a health issue, not a criminal one.
* Fight Mass Incarceration & Police Brutality - Abolish mandatory mins & cash bail; demilitarize police departments
* College For All - Make all public higher education tuition-free with no out-of-pocket cost for room/board & cancel all student loan debt.
* Housing For All - Establish universal rent-control and the guaranteed right to counsel for all tenants. Public investment in building quality multi-income social housing.
* Support Working Families - Expansive paid family leave for all workers, and free public universal childcare and pre-K.
* 32-Hour Work Week - A 32-hour work week with no reduction in pay or benefits.
* Union Power - Protect all workers' rights to strike and form a union, make it easier for workers to join unions, and invest in the National Labor Relations Board
* Tax the Rich - Raise taxes on the richest earners, for-profit corporations, large inheritances, and private colleges and universities. Establish a wealth tax for the wealthiest in society.
* Green New Deal - Massive public investment to transition away from fossil fuels toward a green and sustainable economy.
* Free Palestine - An immediate and permanent ceasefire in Gaza, an end to military and economic aid, and weapons sales to Israel. National sovereignty for the Palestinian people.
* End the US War Machine - Slash the US military budget, close overseas bases, and bring tropps wars.
* No More Economic Warfare - End economic sanctions that impact the sovereignty of countries whose governments act independently of the US, such as Cuba, Venezuela, and Iran.
* Freedom of Movement - Allow workers to freely migrate between countries to seek employment without immigration controls.
* Universal Suffrage - Extend full voting rights to people with criminal convictions and noncitizens & establish statehood for DC.
* An Actually Representative Congress - Replace the two-party system with a multi-party democracy through proportional representation elections. Expand the number of seats in the House & end the Senate filibuster.
* Popular Vote For President - Replace the Electoral College with a national popular vote for President.
* Reduce the Power of the Supreme Court - Limit the Court's power of judicial review which it uses to effectively create and abolish laws outside the legislative process.
